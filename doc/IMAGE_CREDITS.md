## Re-downloading full-size files

The three purchased photos (iStock and Freepik) are the small (about 600 px wide)
copies from the links in the credits sheet. For sharper slides and banners,
re-download the full-size files from the iStock account (My account → Download
history) and Freepik (Profile → Downloads), then replace the files with the same names.
