# 1. Executive Overview

Chase Care Services is a Ghana-based care and support organisation
providing person-centred care, professional support, training, respite
services, community engagement and wellbeing services for individuals,
families, carers and healthcare professionals.

The website should function as more than a standard care-agency website.
It should become the digital home of an integrated care ecosystem
connecting clients and families, caregivers, healthcare professionals,
training programmes, worker wellbeing, respite services, community
partnerships and the Ghanaian diaspora.

The platform should serve people living in Ghana as well as people
living abroad who need trusted care and support arranged for loved ones
in Ghana.

# 2. Vision

To make quality care accessible, excellent, sustainable and
person-centred for everyone who needs it in Ghana and beyond.

Chase Care Services envisions a Ghana where individuals and families can
easily access trusted, professional and compassionate care services,
regardless of age, disability or circumstance. The organisation also
aims to develop a recognised workforce of well-trained caregivers and
care professionals who are confident, competent, supported and valued.

# 3. Mission

To provide high-quality, person-centred care and support while
developing a competent, compassionate and professionally supported care
workforce.

- Match clients with suitable carers and professionals.

- Provide competency-based training before workers begin assignments.

- Support continuous professional development (CPD).

- Provide wellbeing and counselling services for the workforce.

- Connect workers with appropriate health and laboratory services.

- Support families and parents through education and respite.

- Promote inclusion for people living with disabilities.

- Work with schools, hospitals, organisations and community partners.

- Create opportunities for young people to enter the care profession.

- Provide reliable care solutions for Ghanaian families locally and in
  the diaspora.

# 11. Client Registration

Create an online I Need Care registration form. It should collect
sufficient information for ChaseCare to understand the request and
follow up appropriately.

- Personal information: name, contact details, location and country of
  residence.

- Who needs care: self, parent, child, spouse, relative or other.

- Type of support: elderly care, disability, autism, home care, respite,
  weekend care, nanny, housekeeping, therapy, community support,
  hospital support, school support or other.

- Preferred frequency: one-off, daily, weekly, weekends, overnight,
  live-in, short-term or long-term.

- Additional information and care requirements.

After submission: Thank you. A member of the Chase Care Services team
will review your request and contact you.

# 31. Security, Privacy & Data Protection

Because ChaseCare may handle sensitive personal and health-related
information, the website should be designed as a professional
care-management platform rather than a simple brochure website.

- Secure login

- Role-based access

- Encrypted connections

- Secure document uploads

- Password protection

- Two-factor authentication where appropriate

- Audit logs

- Regular backups

- Privacy policy

- Terms of service

- Cookie policy

- Data-retention procedures

- Appropriate consent mechanisms

The final implementation should be reviewed against Ghanaian
data-protection and healthcare-related requirements applicable to the
specific services ChaseCare provides.

The website should communicate three interconnected pillars:

## CARE

Professional, person-centred services for individuals and families.

## CAREERS

Training, employment and professional development for caregivers and
healthcare professionals.

## COMMUNITY

Wellbeing, inclusion, education, partnerships and community engagement.

This gives ChaseCare a stronger identity than simply being a company

# 37. Recommended Next Steps for the Web Designer

- Confirm the brand identity, logo, colours and typography.

- Create the full sitemap and user-flow diagrams.

- Design the homepage and core service-page templates.

- Design the client, worker and administrator portal architecture before
  development begins.

- Define the online registration and application forms.

- Define booking, payment and notification requirements.

- Define the care-reporting and document-upload workflows.

- Confirm hosting, domain, SSL, backup and security arrangements.

- Confirm the payment gateway and communication tools.

- Confirm the privacy, consent and data-management requirements.

- Build Phase 1 first, while ensuring the technical architecture can
  support later phases.

- Test the website on mobile, tablet and desktop before launch.

- Train ChaseCare administrators to manage content, users, courses,
  bookings and reports.

# 38. Conclusion

Chase Care Services has the opportunity to build a distinctive care
platform for Ghana that combines professional care delivery, caregiver
employment, training, family support, respite, wellbeing, community
engagement and diaspora services.

The website should therefore be developed as a scalable platform. It
should begin with a clear, trustworthy public website and progressively
develop into a secure digital care ecosystem with client, worker and
administrator functionality.

The central promise should remain simple: ChaseCare connects people with
quality care, equips care professionals with the right skills and
support, and helps families access dependable care wherever they are.
